# FreshCart Account and Privacy Policy

Version 2.0, effective 1 January 2026.

## 1. Account security

Customers sign in with a one-time password sent to their registered mobile number. FreshCart support will never ask for the OTP, card number, CVV or UPI PIN. Customers should report anyone asking for these details.

## 2. Changing account details

The registered mobile number can be changed from the Profile page after verifying an OTP on both the old and new numbers. Delivery addresses can be added or edited at any time, but changes to an address made after 10 PM apply from the delivery after next.

## 3. Data FreshCart collects

FreshCart collects name, mobile number, delivery addresses, order history and payment status. Full card numbers are never stored by FreshCart; they are tokenised by the payment gateway.

## 4. Data retention

Order and invoice records are kept for 8 years to meet tax law. Chat transcripts with support are kept for 1 year. Location data is used only during active deliveries and is not stored after the order is completed.

## 5. Account deletion

Customers can ask for account deletion from the Profile page or through support. Deletion is completed within 30 days. Before deletion, any wallet cash balance is refunded to the customer's verified bank account. Promotional balance is forfeited. Active subscriptions must be cancelled first.

## 6. Sharing support conversations

Support agents can see a customer's orders, wallet transactions and subscriptions only for the customer they are currently helping. Agents and automated assistants must not reveal one customer's information to another customer.
