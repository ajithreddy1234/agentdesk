# FreshCart Freshness and Quality Guarantee

Version 1.4, effective 1 June 2025.

## 1. Our promise

Every dairy product is delivered with at least 2 days of shelf life remaining, and packaged goods with at least one third of their total shelf life remaining. Milk is stored at 4 degrees Celsius or below from the dairy to the customer's door.

## 2. Cold chain

Milk and dairy products travel in insulated crates with ice packs. Delivery partners must keep crates closed between drops and complete a morning route within 150 minutes of leaving the hub.

## 3. Sourcing

Milk is sourced from partner farms within 150 km of each city hub. Every batch is tested for adulterants, antibiotics and fat content before it is packed. Batch numbers are printed on every pouch so issues can be traced back to the farm.

## 4. Quality complaints

If a product does not meet this guarantee, the customer is entitled to a full refund for the affected units under the Returns and Refunds Policy. For spoiled milk, FreshCart also offers a free replacement in the next morning slot when the customer asks for it.

## 5. Food safety incidents

If a customer reports illness that they believe is linked to a FreshCart product, the complaint is escalated immediately to the Quality team. Support agents must not offer medical advice and must share the food safety helpline: 1800-123-9999.
