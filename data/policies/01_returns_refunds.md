# FreshCart Returns and Refunds Policy

Version 3.2, effective 1 April 2026. Applies to all orders placed on the FreshCart app and website.

## 1. Scope

This policy explains when a customer can return an item, how refunds are calculated and how long they take. It covers groceries, dairy, fresh produce, bakery items and packaged goods sold by FreshCart. Items sold by marketplace partners follow the partner's own policy, which is linked on the product page.

## 2. Return windows

Perishable items such as milk, curd, paneer, bread, fruits and vegetables must be reported within 24 hours of delivery. Customers report them by raising a complaint in the app with a photo of the item. Physical return of perishable items is not required.

Packaged and non-perishable items such as rice, atta, dal, oil and beverages can be returned within 7 days of delivery if they are unopened and in their original packaging.

Items marked "Final Sale" on the product page cannot be returned unless they arrive damaged or expired.

## 3. Eligible reasons

A return or refund is accepted when the item arrived damaged, spoiled, expired, leaking, or is different from what was ordered. A refund is also accepted when an item was missing from the delivery. Change of mind is accepted only for unopened non-perishable items within the 7-day window.

## 4. How refunds are paid

By default, refunds are credited to the FreshCart Wallet within 2 hours of the complaint being approved. Customers can instead ask for a refund to the original payment method. Refunds to UPI are completed within 2 business days, and refunds to credit or debit cards within 5 to 7 business days. Cash on delivery orders can only be refunded to the FreshCart Wallet or a bank account added by the customer.

## 5. Automatic approval limits

Complaints for a refund value up to Rs 500 per order are approved automatically when the customer's account is in good standing. Complaints above Rs 500, or a third complaint within 30 days, are reviewed by the support team within 24 hours. A human agent must approve every refund above Rs 2,000.

## 6. Partial and full refunds

When only some units in an order are affected, FreshCart refunds only those units at the price paid, including any discount applied. Delivery fees are refunded only when the whole order is cancelled by FreshCart or when every item in the order is refunded.

## 7. Abuse prevention

Accounts with more than 5 approved refund complaints in a 60-day period are flagged for review. FreshCart may ask for additional photos, suspend automatic approvals, or close accounts that repeatedly misuse the refund process.

## 8. Contact

Customers can raise a complaint from the order page in the app, by chat, or by calling 1800-123-4567 between 6 AM and 10 PM.
