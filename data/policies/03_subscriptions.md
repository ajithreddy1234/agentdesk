# FreshCart Subscriptions Terms

Version 1.9, effective 1 March 2026.

## 1. What a subscription is

A subscription is a recurring morning delivery of the same items, such as one litre of toned milk every day. Customers choose the frequency: daily, alternate days, weekdays only, or a custom weekly pattern.

## 2. Prepaid wallet requirement

Subscriptions are paid from the FreshCart Wallet. The wallet must hold enough balance for the next delivery by 10 PM on the previous day. If the balance is too low, the next delivery is skipped and the customer receives a reminder notification. After 3 consecutive skipped deliveries, the subscription is paused automatically.

## 3. Changing or pausing a subscription

Quantity, frequency and items can be changed until 10 PM on the previous day. A subscription can be paused for any number of days and resumed at any time. Paused days are not charged.

## 4. Subscription pricing

Subscription prices are locked for 30 days from the start date. After that, the current catalogue price applies, and customers are notified 7 days before any price increase. Subscribers receive a 5% discount on milk and curd compared with one-time orders.

## 5. FreshCart Plus

FreshCart Plus is a paid membership that costs Rs 149 per month or Rs 1,199 per year. Benefits include free express delivery, an extra 5% cashback in the wallet on every order, and priority customer support. The membership renews automatically and can be cancelled at any time. On cancellation, benefits continue until the end of the paid period. The monthly fee is refundable only if cancelled within 48 hours of purchase and no benefit has been used.

## 6. Cancellation

A subscription can be cancelled from the Subscriptions page at any time. Any unused wallet balance stays in the wallet and can be used for other orders or withdrawn as described in the Payments and Wallet Policy.
