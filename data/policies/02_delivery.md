# FreshCart Delivery Policy

Version 2.4, effective 15 January 2026.

## 1. Delivery areas

FreshCart delivers in Hyderabad, Bengaluru, Mumbai, Delhi NCR, Chennai and Pune. Customers can check whether their pin code is serviceable on the app home screen before placing an order.

## 2. Delivery slots

Morning slot orders are delivered between 5 AM and 7:30 AM. They must be placed before 10 PM on the previous day. Express delivery is available from 8 AM to 9 PM and arrives within 30 minutes in eligible pin codes. Scheduled evening slots run from 5 PM to 8 PM.

## 3. Delivery fees

Morning slot delivery is free for all orders. Express delivery costs Rs 25 for orders below Rs 199 and is free for orders of Rs 199 or more. FreshCart Plus members get free express delivery on every order. A small-cart fee of Rs 15 applies to scheduled evening orders below Rs 99.

## 4. Late and failed deliveries

If a morning slot order arrives after 7:30 AM, the customer receives a Rs 50 wallet credit automatically. If an express order arrives more than 15 minutes late, the delivery fee is refunded. Each account can receive at most 4 late-delivery credits per calendar month.

If the delivery partner cannot reach the customer, perishable items are left at the door only when the customer has enabled "Leave at door". Otherwise, the order is marked as failed. A failed delivery caused by the customer is not refunded for perishable items.

## 5. Order changes and cancellations

Morning slot orders can be edited or cancelled until 11 PM the night before delivery. Express orders can be cancelled free of charge within 2 minutes of placing them, or before a delivery partner is assigned, whichever comes first. After that, a cancellation fee of Rs 20 applies.

## 6. Vacation mode

Customers can pause all deliveries for up to 30 days using Vacation Mode. Subscriptions resume automatically on the end date.
