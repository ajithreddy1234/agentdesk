# FreshCart Payments and Wallet Policy

Version 2.1, effective 10 February 2026.

## 1. Accepted payment methods

FreshCart accepts UPI, credit and debit cards, net banking, the FreshCart Wallet and cash on delivery. Cash on delivery is available only for orders up to Rs 2,000 and is not available for subscriptions.

## 2. FreshCart Wallet

The wallet holds two kinds of balance. Cash balance comes from money the customer adds and from refunds. Promotional balance comes from cashback, referral rewards and goodwill credits.

Cash balance never expires. Promotional balance expires 90 days after it is credited. At most 10% of an order's value can be paid with promotional balance.

The maximum cash balance a wallet can hold is Rs 10,000, as required for small prepaid payment instruments.

## 3. Withdrawing wallet balance

Customers with a completed KYC can withdraw cash balance to their verified bank account. Withdrawals are processed within 3 business days. Promotional balance cannot be withdrawn. Withdrawals are free up to twice a month; after that a fee of Rs 10 applies per withdrawal.

## 4. Failed payments

If money is debited but the order is not placed, the amount is refunded to the original payment method automatically within 48 hours. Customers can also ask support to credit it to the wallet instantly instead.

## 5. Referral rewards

When a referred friend completes their first order of Rs 300 or more, both customers receive Rs 100 promotional balance. Each customer can earn referral rewards for at most 10 friends.
